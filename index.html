This site builds its maps and charts from the most up-to-date publicly available data, covering ward boundaries, voting districts and stations, and census figures.

Please note that the Electoral Commission (IEC) is actively and continuously updating its voting district data, so not all voting districts shown here are fully up to date yet.

This site is not official and is not endorsed by the Municipal Demarcation Board, the Electoral Commission (IEC), Stellenbosch Municipality, or any other data provider. It is a citizen’s effort, compiled with care from those providers’ own published data. For anything official, please confirm with the relevant authority.
